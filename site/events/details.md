# Event details and replies

Click an event to see its details in the right panel.

![An event selected, with its details in the right panel and edit and delete buttons above them](../../assets/images/details.png)

The details show the time, the calendar, the location, the meeting link, the guests and how each one replied (with any notes they left), the description, whether you show as Busy or Free, and the event's visibility.

At the top of the panel, the pencil button opens the [editor](../editing/) and the trash button deletes the event. Below the details, the panel lists the keys you can press for the selected event.

## Joining a meeting

When an event has a meeting link, the details show a **Join** button. Google Meet links open with the right Google account already picked, so you never land in a meeting signed in as the wrong person. Zoom, Teams, and Webex links open in their apps when they're installed.

Click the arrow next to **Join** (**More join options**) to **Copy link** instead.

| Keys | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl+J` | Join the selected meeting, or your next one |
| `V` | Open the meeting link in your browser |

See [Joining meetings](../../tray-and-notifications/joining/) for joining from anywhere in Windows.

## Replying to an invitation

Under **Your response**, pick **Going**, **Maybe**, or **Not going**. You can **Add a note (optional)** to go with your reply.

You can also reply by:

- Right-clicking the event.
- Pressing `E` then `Y` (yes), `N` (no), or `M` (maybe).
- Clicking **Yes**, **No**, or **Maybe** on the [invitation notification](../../tray-and-notifications/notifications/).

> If the same meeting is on the calendars of several of your accounts, Leaf Calendar shows it once.

## Emailing guests

Click **Email guests** (or press `E` then `E`) to start an email to everyone on the event in your mail app. If you're a guest rather than the organizer, **Email the organizer** writes to the person who invited you.

## Locations

Click the location to open it in Google Maps or Bing Maps. Pick which one in **Settings › General › Open locations in**.

## Right-click menu

Right-click any event for quick actions, like replying, changing its color, copying, and deleting.

![An event's right-click menu](../../assets/images/event-menu.png)

## Seeing who's free

With a meeting selected, press `E` then `F` to lay every guest's busy times over your calendar. See [People and busy times](../../people-and-scheduling/people/).
