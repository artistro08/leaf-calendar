# Sign in for the first time

The first time you open Leaf Calendar, a small setup window walks you through connecting Google. The line-shaped dots at the bottom show which step you're on.

## 1. Welcome

![The welcome step of the setup window, with a Get started button](../../assets/images/onboarding-welcome.png)

Click **Get started**.

## 2. Connect your Google Cloud client

![The OAuth client step, with a link to the Google Cloud setup guide and boxes for the Client ID and Client secret](../../assets/images/onboarding-client.png)

Paste the **Client ID** and **Client secret** from your OAuth client, then click **Next**. Haven't made one yet? Follow the [Google Cloud setup guide](../google-cloud-setup/) first, and don't forget to [add yourself as a test user](../google-cloud-setup/#4-add-yourself-as-a-test-user).

Leaf Calendar keeps your Client ID and Client secret encrypted on your PC. You only enter them once.

## 3. Sign in with Google

Click **Sign in with Google**. Your browser opens Google's sign-in page.

1. Pick the Google account you added as a test user.
2. If Google says it **hasn't verified this app**, click **Advanced**, then **Go to (your app name)**. It's your own app, so this is safe.
3. Allow Leaf Calendar to see and edit your calendars and to see your contacts.
4. When the browser says you can close the tab, close it and go back to Leaf Calendar. The page also offers an **Open Leaf Calendar** link that brings the app to the front.

If sign-in doesn't finish, Leaf Calendar shows **Try again**. See [troubleshooting](../google-cloud-setup/#troubleshooting) if Google shows an error.

## 4. Syncing your calendars

Leaf Calendar downloads your calendars and events. You'll see how many it has found so far. This can take a minute for a busy calendar, and it only happens once. After that, Leaf Calendar only fetches what changed.

## 5. Schedule with Cal.com (optional)

If you use [Cal.com](https://cal.com), you can connect it here so you can share your free times as booking links. Click **Skip** if you don't use Cal.com. You can always set it up later in **Settings › Accounts**. See [Cal.com booking links](../../people-and-scheduling/cal-com/) for how to set it up.

## 6. You're all set

Click **Open Leaf Calendar**. The setup window closes and your calendar opens.

> If you close the setup window before you finish, Leaf Calendar asks if you want to leave setup. Leaving without signing in to any account closes Leaf Calendar. Open it again whenever you're ready to pick up where you left off.

## Adding more Google accounts

Leaf Calendar can show as many Google accounts as you like, side by side. Go to **Settings › Accounts** and click **Add Google account**. Each account you add must also be a [test user](../google-cloud-setup/#4-add-yourself-as-a-test-user) on your OAuth client (unless you've published it).
