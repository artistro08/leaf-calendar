# Getting started

Three steps get you from nothing to your calendar on screen:

1. [Install Leaf Calendar](install/) from the latest release.
2. [Set up your Google Cloud OAuth client](google-cloud-setup/), including adding yourself as a test user.
3. [Open Leaf Calendar and sign in](first-run/). The setup window walks you through the rest.

### Prerequisites

- Windows 11 (x64)
- A Google account
- About 10 minutes for the one-time Google Cloud setup
