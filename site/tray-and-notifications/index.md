# Tray and notifications

Leaf Calendar lives in your system tray, so your next meeting is always close, even with the main window closed.

- [The tray icon](tray/): hover for your next meeting, click for an agenda, right-click for a menu.
- [Notifications](notifications/): reminders, "Join now", invitations, and more.
- [Joining meetings](joining/): one shortcut joins your next meeting with the right Google account.

> Closing the main window keeps Leaf Calendar running in the tray, so reminders still arrive. To fully close it, choose **Quit** from the tray menu.
