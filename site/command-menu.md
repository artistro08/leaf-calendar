# Command menu and search

The command menu is the fastest way to do almost anything in Leaf Calendar. Search your events, jump to a date, or run any action, all without leaving the keyboard.

## Opening it

- Press `Ctrl+K`.
- Press `Ctrl+F` or `/` to search.
- Click the search icon at the top of the sidebar.

![The command menu open at the top of the window, listing actions](../assets/images/command-menu.png)

Type to filter, use `↑` and `↓` to pick a result, and press `Enter` to run it. `Esc` closes the menu.

## Searching events

Type part of an event's title, description, guest, or location. Leaf Calendar searches everything on your PC, past and future, so it works offline too.

![Searching for "dent" finds the Dentist event](../assets/images/search-events.png)

- `Enter` opens the event's details.
- `Alt+Enter` jumps the calendar to the event.
- For a repeating event, the result is its next one.

## Going to a date

Type a date in plain words, and the menu offers to go there:

- "oct 15"
- "next friday"
- "in 2 weeks", "two weeks from now"
- "3 days ago"

![Typing "next friday" offers to go to that date](../assets/images/command-date.png)

Press `.` on the calendar to open the menu ready for a date (**Jump to date**).

## Running actions

Type what you want to do. Some of the things you can run:

- **Create event** (type a title, then pick **Create event "…"**)
- Go to today, switch views (day, week, month)
- **Join** your next meeting
- Overlay a teammate's calendar, or **Meet with** people
- Time travel to another time zone
- Share availability
- Sync now
- Switch between light and dark, show or hide weekends and declined events
- Open any page of Settings
- Show keyboard shortcuts

![Typing "settings" lists every Settings page](../assets/images/command-settings.png)

If nothing matches, the menu says "No events, actions, or dates match."
