# Sidebar and calendars

The sidebar sits on the left of the main window. Click the sidebar button in the top-left corner to show or hide it.

![The sidebar with the mini month at the top and calendars listed by account below it](../../assets/images/main-week.png)

## The mini month

A small month with today highlighted. Click any date to jump to it, and use the arrows to see other months. The search icon at the top opens the [command menu](../../command-menu/).

## Your calendars

Every Google account you've signed in to is listed with its calendars, including the ones you're subscribed to in Google Calendar.

- **Check or uncheck a calendar** to show or hide its events. The checkbox uses the calendar's color.
- **Right-click a calendar** for more:
  - **Rename…** gives the calendar a name only you see, in Leaf Calendar and in Google Calendar. Leave the name empty to go back to the calendar's own name.
  - **Show upcoming events** lists that calendar's events for the next 30 days in the details panel.
  - **Change color…** opens **Settings › Calendars**.

![The Rename calendar dialog with a box for the new name](../../assets/images/rename-calendar.png)

> Renaming a calendar needs an internet connection, since the new name is saved to Google.

## Sunsama

When you're [signed in to Sunsama](../sunsama/), a **Sunsama** group shows under your Google accounts with two items. Check or uncheck them like calendars:

- **Projected tasks** shows or hides your Sunsama task blocks.
- **Tracked time** shows or hides the time you've tracked on tasks.

![The sidebar's Sunsama group with Projected tasks and Tracked time](../../assets/images/sunsama-sidebar.png)

## The buttons at the bottom

- **Settings** (the gear) opens [Settings](../../settings/). You can also press `Ctrl+,`.
- **Share availability** starts [sharing your free times](../../people-and-scheduling/share-availability/). You can also press `S`.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** opens the [shortcut cheat sheet](../../keyboard-shortcuts/). You can also press `?`.

## Colors, order, and reminders

Calendar colors, the order calendars are listed in, and each calendar's default reminders live in [Settings › Calendars](../../settings/#calendars).
