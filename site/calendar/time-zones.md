# Time zones

Leaf Calendar shows your calendar in one primary time zone, and can show up to 4 extra time zone columns beside it, so you can see what time it is for the people you work with.

## Your primary time zone

By default, Leaf Calendar follows Windows' time zone, so your calendar changes when you travel.

If you've picked a time zone of your own and your PC's time zone changes (say, you land in London), a bar asks "Your PC is now on London time. Show Leaf Calendar in that time zone?" Click **Switch** to follow it, or **Keep** (it names your own zone, like "Keep Chicago") to stay put. Turn this question off with **Ask when the Windows time zone changes**.

To pick a time zone of your own, go to **Settings › Time zones**, open **Primary time zone**, and turn off **Use Windows time zone**.

![Settings › Time zones with the primary time zone open and the extra time zones listed](../../assets/images/settings-timezones.png)

## Extra time zone columns

1. Go to **Settings › Time zones**.
2. In **Add a time zone**, type a city or a short name, like Tokyo, NYC, SF, LON, or UTC. Small typos are fine: "Pheonix" still finds Phoenix.
3. Pick the zone from the list.

Each extra zone shows as a column of hours to the left of the calendar. Under **Your time zones**, give each column a label of your own, drag the zones (or use **More options › Move up** and **Move down**) to change their order, and **Remove** one you don't need.

## Time travel

Want to see your whole calendar as someone in another time zone sees it? Press `Z`, or search for "time travel" in the [command menu](../../command-menu/), and pick a zone.

![The time travel bar at the top of the calendar, with a Return button](../../assets/images/time-travel.png)

The calendar redraws in that zone, and a bar above it says "Viewing your calendar in Tokyo time (JST)." Click **Return** to go back to your own time zone.

## An event's own time zone

Each event can have its own time zone, which helps for flights and trips. In the [event editor](../../events/editing/), change **Time zone**, or press `E` then `Z` with an event selected.
