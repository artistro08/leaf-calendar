# Views and navigation

## Picking a view

Use the view picker in the title bar, a keyboard shortcut, or the [command menu](../../command-menu/).

![The view picker open, listing Day, Week, Month, and the custom days view](../../assets/images/view-menu.png)

| View | Shortcut | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day | `D` or `1` | One day, hour by hour |
| Week | `W` or `0` | One week, hour by hour |
| Month | `M` | A whole month, with events listed in each day |
| Custom days | `2` to `9` | That many days side by side |

Set the length of the custom view (from 1 to 31 days) in **Settings › General › Days in the custom view**. Leaf Calendar remembers the last view you used.

## Moving around

- **Today** (`T`) jumps to today.
- **Previous** and **Next** (`←` and `→`, or `K` and `J`) move one period at a time.
- **Go to date** (`.`) opens the command menu ready for a date. Type something like "oct 12", "next fri", or "in 2 weeks" and press Enter.
- **Click a day in the mini month** in the sidebar to jump to it.
- **Scroll sideways** with a trackpad, Shift and the mouse wheel, or by dragging, to slide smoothly through the days.
- **Next event** (`N`) and **previous event** (`B` or `Shift+N`) select the next or previous event.
- **Back and forward** (`Alt+←` and `Alt+→`, or your mouse's back and forward buttons) step through the places you've looked at.

## What's on the grid

- **The current-time line** marks right now.
- **Working hours** are shaded so you can see when you're working. Google doesn't share your working hours with apps, so you set them in **Settings › General › Working hours** (9 AM to 5 PM, Monday to Friday, until you change them).
- **The all-day row** sits above the hours. Click it to expand or collapse it. Multi-day events keep their titles visible as you scroll.
- **Past events** fade so today's remaining events stand out.
- **Focus time, out of office, and birthday** events each have their own look.

## Changing what you see

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl+=` / `Ctrl+-` | Make hours taller or shorter (Ctrl and the mouse wheel over the grid works too) |
| `Ctrl+0` | Reset the hour height |
| `Ctrl+Shift+E` | Show or hide weekends |
| `Ctrl+Shift+D` | Show or hide events you declined |
| `Ctrl+Shift+L` | Switch between light and dark |

You'll find the same options, plus week numbers, the first day of the week, and 24-hour time, in [Settings › General](../../settings/#general).
