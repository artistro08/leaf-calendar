# People and busy times

See when other people are busy, right on your own calendar, so you can pick a time that works for everyone.

> You see what Google lets you see. For most coworkers that's just **busy** blocks. If someone shares their calendar with you with details, you'll see more. Leaf Calendar asks Google with your main account (**Settings › Accounts › Main account**).

## Overlay a teammate (P)

1. Press `P`, or search for "overlay" in the [command menu](../../command-menu/).
2. Type a name or email and pick the person.

![The people picker, with a box for a name or email](../../assets/images/people-picker.png)

Their busy times appear on your calendar next to your events. You can overlay up to 20 people at once.

## Meet with (F)

1. Press `F`, or search for "meet with" in the command menu.
2. Pick one or more people.
3. Look for a gap where nobody is busy, and drag on the calendar to create the event. Everyone you picked is already added as a guest.

![The Meet with picker, ready for names](../../assets/images/meet-with.png)

## The guests of a meeting (E then F)

Select a meeting and press `E` then `F` to overlay every guest's busy times. Handy when you need to move a meeting.

## The overlay bar

While anyone is overlaid, a bar at the bottom of the calendar shows who:

- One person shows as a chip with a remove button.
- Two or more show as a dropdown ("3 people"), each with its own remove button.
- **Clear** removes everyone.

![The overlay bar at the bottom of the calendar, listing the people overlaid with a Clear button](../../assets/images/overlay-bar.png)
