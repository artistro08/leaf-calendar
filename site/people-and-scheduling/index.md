# People and scheduling

Find a time that works with other people, and offer them your free times.

- [People and busy times](people/): lay a teammate's busy times over your calendar, or pick several people and find a time together.
- [Share availability](share-availability/): drag the times you're free and copy them as text, then book the one they pick.
- [Cal.com booking links](cal-com/): connect Cal.com so each time you share is a link people can book on their own.
